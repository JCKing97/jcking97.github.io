This was seriously good.<!--end of excerpt--> I really tried to enjoy Braiding Sweetgrass, but found myself wading through its structure and repetitiveness. It contained absolutely profound points and beautifully poetic writing, but I didn't enjoy it or feel connected to it.

<div style="text-align: center;">  
    <img alt="Book Front Cover" width="30%" src="/assets/images/blog/{{ page.img }}"/>
</div>

Gathering Moss felt like the book I wanted Braiding Sweetgrass to be. You still get Robin's insightful perspective and flowing writing. But this time, I felt like I was being drawn down into the perspective of the moss with every page.

Robin makes you feel like you're accompanying her uncovering the life of mosses rather than being taught about it. You almost end up feeling you are connected to the web of reciprocity she has built. But you become certain that you can adopt some of her attention and kindness to weave some for your own life.
