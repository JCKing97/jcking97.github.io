Moths are members of the lepidoptera family that also includes butterflies. There is a far wider variety of moths than butterflies with over 2500 species recorded in the UK in comparison to the butterflies 60 recorded species.

Moths are often seen as the less vibrant of the lepidotera, but tell that to the caterpillar of the Lackey. The caterpillar stage of the moth lifecycle is the second stage. It first spends time as an egg, then in the larval form of a caterpillar, then as a pupa whilst it pupates to finally become an adult moth.

The body of the moth is completely rebuilt between the juvenile and adult forms in the process of metamorphosis.
