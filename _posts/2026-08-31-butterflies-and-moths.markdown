    Lepidoptera is an order containing winged insects including butterflies and moths. Butterflies being some of the most colourful species in nature. Pictured is one of the less colourful species the <span class="species-name">Meadow Brown</span> at Brede High Woods in East Sussex.<br/><br/>

    There are some species which migrate incredibly long distances such as the Monarch in the Americas and the Painted Lady in Africa and Europe. There arrival heralds the change of seasons and in some cases such as the Orange-Tip the brighter days of spring.<br/><br/>

    Moths are often considered less charismatic but consider the six-spot burnet or elephant hawk moth and you might change your mind. They're also incredibly important pollinators of well-known plants such as peas, strawberries and honeysuckle.
