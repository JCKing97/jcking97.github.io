These predatory flying insects make up the Odonata order, whose fossils first appear in the late Triassic over 200 Ma (million years ago).<br/><br/>

They are extremely agile, catching prey on the wing and are a key health indicator species of their chosen habitat, wetlands.<br/><br/>

There are 57 species of Odonata in the UK and this species, the <a href="https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396420381"><span class="species-name">Emperor Dragonfly<i class="fa-solid fa-dove" style="color: #CD7F32;" aria-hidden="true"></i></span></a>, was photographed at Broadwater Warren in East Sussex.
