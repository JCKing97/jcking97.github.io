Finches are a family of passerines (perching birds) made up of some very well known species including Greenfinches, Bullfinches and Hawfinches.<br/><br/>

Finches are very widely spread across the world, including the inhabitants of the galapagos that Charles Darwin studied. Whilst those mentioned above inhabit the UK and are spread through Europe and Asia.<br/><br/>

The pictured bird is a <a href="https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/373326680"><span class="species-name">Common Linnet<i class="fa-solid fa-dove" style="color: #CD7F32;" aria-hidden="true"></i></span></a> at Rye Harbour Nature Reserve. You can tell from the gorgeous reddy-pinky breast that this is a male in breeding plumage.
