Welcome to my portfolio! I am a Software Engineer at the Natural History Museum in London. I have always enjoyed walking in nature, but a few years back I read Wilding by Isabella Tree which opened my eyes to a deeper appreciation.

Since then I have found more ways to connect with the characters that inhabit our world. Starting with spending time in local biodiversity hotspot and reading all the nature writing I could. I've taken a botany course and foraged. Creating tasty food and drink with wild garlic, blackberries, elderflower etc.

In 2025 I was lucky that an amazing person introduced me to photography. It has taken me a step closer to characters I have walked alongside for years.

This portfolio aims to explore the characters and ecology I have been observing in even more depth. I'll be presenting the photographs and my throughts in an accessible reader and listener-friendly form. My hope is to:

  • Showcase these characters
  • Write creatively
  • Gain a deeper understanding
  • Share my love
